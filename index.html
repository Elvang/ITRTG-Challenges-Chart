    Challenge types

      Reading a box

      • ▶ is the guide's first instruction. Small boxes further along the line say when to come back.
      • ↻ repeat means the guide says to keep coming back to it.
      • ★★★ in the header is how useful the guide rates the reward, from 1 to 5 stars.
      • The bars show the guide's playstyle rating:
        Lazy Moderate Semi-active Active

      Moving around

      • Drag to pan. Ctrl + scroll or pinch to zoom. Press / to search.
      • Click a box for details, guides and calculators.

      Imported stats

      • Solid header: you can start it. Hollow header: locked. Hollow with a dashed border: the export can't confirm the unlock, so check it yourself. Faded: maxed.
      • Your export stays in this browser only.

      Sources

        Import your stats

        In game, copy your statistics export and paste all of it here. It starts with “Idling to Rule the Gods - statistics export”. Challenges you've maxed fade out, and each box shows your count or whether you can start it yet.
